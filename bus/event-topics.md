# Event Bus — Topics

Defined bus topics and intent:
- `trend.found` — Trend Research published a signal with evidence.
- `product.idea` — Product Factory or Product agent proposes a new idea.
- `product.validated` — A product idea is validated for a next step.
- `etsy.draft_needed` — An Etsy listing draft is requested.
- `etsy.draft_ready` — An Etsy draft is prepared and ready for owner review.
- `ad.copy_needed` — Request for ad/copy variations.
- `finance.margin_check` — Request or result for margin and pricing checks.
- `compliance.review_needed` — Compliance review requested for a draft or product.
- `support.reply_needed` — Support reply draft requested.
- `buroq.website_task` — Website-specific task to integrate assets or content.
- `design.asset_needed` — Request for design assets or mockups.
- `human.approval_required` — Explicit owner approval required to proceed.
